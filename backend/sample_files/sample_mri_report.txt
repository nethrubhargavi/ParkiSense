MRI BRAIN REPORT
=================
Patient: John Doe
Date: 2026-02-27
Modality: MRI Brain with and without contrast (Gadolinium)

TECHNIQUE:
Axial, sagittal, and coronal T1 weighted, T2 weighted, FLAIR, and diffusion-weighted sequences were obtained. Post-contrast T1 weighted images were acquired following intravenous administration of gadolinium.

FINDINGS:
1. There is mild generalized cerebral cortical atrophy noted, particularly in the frontal and temporal lobes, slightly disproportionate to age.
2. The basal ganglia demonstrate subtle signal changes on T2 weighted images. The putamen shows mild volume loss bilaterally.
3. The substantia nigra shows reduced signal on T2* weighted imaging compared to age-matched controls, suggestive of possible dopaminergic pathway involvement.
4. Scattered periventricular and subcortical white matter hyperintensities on FLAIR sequences, consistent with mild small vessel ischemic disease.
5. The caudate nucleus appears normal in size and signal.
6. The thalamus, cerebellum, and brainstem are unremarkable.
7. No acute infarct on diffusion weighted imaging. No hemorrhage.
8. Ventricular system is mildly prominent but consistent with cortical atrophy.
9. No mass lesion or midline shift. No abnormal enhancement post-contrast.

IMPRESSION:
- Mild cortical atrophy predominantly frontotemporal, disproportionate to patient age.
- Subtle basal ganglia and substantia nigra changes that may be relevant in the clinical context of suspected neurodegenerative disease or Parkinson's disease.
- Mild small vessel white matter changes.
- No acute pathology. No mass or hemorrhage.

RECOMMENDATION:
Clinical correlation with neurological examination recommended. Consider DAT scan for further evaluation of nigrostriatal dopaminergic pathway if clinically indicated.
